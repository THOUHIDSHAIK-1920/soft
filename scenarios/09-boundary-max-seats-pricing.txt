Scenario: Boundary Validation for Exact 6-Seat Maximum Order
Module: SeatMap
Tags: @regression

On the Seat Map page for Quantum Horizon (ST-201, $12.00 per ticket), selecting exactly 6 available seats (G1, G2, G3, G4, G5, G6) is permitted as the upper boundary, updates selected count to 6, calculates total price as $72.00, and enables Proceed to Payment.
