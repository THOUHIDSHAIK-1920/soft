Scenario: Showtime and Theatre Selection with Sold-Out Validation
Module: Showtime
Tags: @regression

On the Showtime Selection page for "Interstellar Odyssey", multiple theatres and screening times are listed, including an available IMAX screening (ST-101) and a Sold Out screening (ST-103).
The Sold Out showtime button must be disabled and non-selectable, while clicking Choose Seats on the available showtime navigates to the 10x10 seat map.
